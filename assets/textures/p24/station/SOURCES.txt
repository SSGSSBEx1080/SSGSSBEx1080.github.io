Vanilla textures for the №24 station diorama: original Minecraft Java Edition 1.19.2 client.jar (Mojang), assets/minecraft/textures/block/*.png and textures/entity/signs/oak.png. lantern.png is the FIRST unmodified 16×16 frame of Minecraft's animated 16×48 block/lantern.png. The lantern geometry and UV coordinates in pages/24-scooter/station3d.js follow the original models/block/template_hanging_lantern.json. Charging port textures are NOT replaced: they remain byte-for-byte identical to mod-src/textures/block/charging_port_*.png in assets/textures/p24/.
